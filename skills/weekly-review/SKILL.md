# Weekly review skill

Run once a week. Produces a short, structured review from the brain —
never a questionnaire, never filler. Silent when there is nothing
material to report.

## Inputs

1. Read `MUSE.md` and `MAP.md` for the current map.
2. Read the open threads in the notes — whatever your notes actually
   track (projects, goals, money, people, admin). Do not invent
   areas you have no notes for; review what is there.
3. Money numbers come from you or a fresh verified read — never from
   memory older than the last verified figure. If a number is stale, say
   so instead of repeating it as current.

## Checks

- **Projects:** what moved, what is stuck, what is awaiting your call.
- **Goals:** progress against the goal, next step, anything blocked.
- **Money:** balances vs the plan, upcoming due dates — verify before
  reporting, never repeat a stale figure as current.
- **People:** upcoming visits or milestones, anything owed.
- **Admin:** paperwork, renewals, calls owed, deadlines.
- **Stale pages:** notes not touched in a while, orphans, broken links.
- **Conflicts:** pages that disagree — flag, do not resolve quietly.
- **Duplicates:** run `scripts/dup-check.py`; link, do not re-file.

## Output

A short review with one section per area above that has anything to
say, each no more than a few lines: what moved, what is stuck, what
needs you. End with at most three items that need your call. Log the
review as a `[[journal]]` note dated that day.

## Exclusions

- Health is excluded by design — it lives in your private health
  records, not in the brain. Never pull clinical detail into the review.
- Nothing in the review authorizes any action: no messages, no bookings,
  no payments, no submissions. It reports; you decide.

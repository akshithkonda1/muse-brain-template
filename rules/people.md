# People rules

Synced rules for every person in the brain. The `notes/` people notes hold
the facts; this file holds how to treat them.

## Names

- Use names exactly as you gave them. Never invent or guess a last
  name. Where you withhold one, the note says so.
- Never guess a full name, a first name, or a contact detail that is
  not on record.

## Contact

- NEVER message anyone without your explicit permission.
- Never surface a person's contact details outside the brain.
- Work people stay in work contexts. Never end-run a chain of command
  or raise a sensitive move through the wrong person.

## Corrections

- When you correct the record, fix only the item you corrected and
  name it explicitly. Never infer from a pronoun.
- When a spoken fragment is unclear, ask plainly what you meant. Claim no
  task, name, or follow-up until you confirm it.

## Sensitive boundaries

- Health detail stays out of the brain by design. Keep clinical detail
  in your private health records, not here.
- Relationship data is sensitive. Keep details in the brain; don't
  surface them casually in other contexts.
- A retired matter stays retired. Do not raise it unless you bring it
  up yourself.

## Judgment

- People are described as you describe them, with your words kept
  verbatim where they carry the meaning. Evaluations rest on your
  complete actual data, not generalized averages. If information is
  missing, name the gap; you will supply corrections in all directions.
